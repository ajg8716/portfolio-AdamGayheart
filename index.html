About

I'm a game programmer with a B.S. in Game Design and Development from the Rochester Institute of Technology. I've played games my whole life, so making them felt natural. I like building physics-based, complex systems: movement controllers, enemy AI, and save systems. With additional skills in narrative writing.

Since May 2025, I've led the team behind State of Entropy, a funded, studio-style production. Also developing its movement, AI, and save systems. I lead by setting realistic goals. Then I schedule milestones as testable benchmarks to reach them. I have a general understanding of pipelines and skills in each discipline, which helps me communicate across the team. I promote a culture where everyone contributes creatively, so we can all take pride in what we ship.

I'm looking for a gameplay programming role at a studio where I can grow as an engineer. I also want to further develop my leadership skills. Take responsibility for leading cycles to deliver quality products.
