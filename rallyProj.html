The goal

Create a car controller from scratch that simulates rally-car physics to practice Unity scripting and build a universal controller that works across multiple cars. It's a proof of concept for a full racing game.

What it shows

This project builds vehicle physics from first principles instead of relying on Unity's wheel colliders.

  • Spring-damper: Each wheel raycasts to the ground, measures its spring compression, and applies a force from that compression, minus a damping term based on the wheel’s velocity.
  • Force-based driving: Acceleration is applied at a point on the car, and the steering torque scales with a turning curve driven by the car’s speed.
  • Grip: A sideways drag force cancels lateral velocity in the car’s local space, so the car doesn’t slide as if on ice.
  • Tunable by design: Every setting is a serialized field, so each car can be tuned in the editor without touching code.

What I would change

This is a proof of concept, and several parts need work before it feels like a rally car:

  • Add a grip model with slip so the car handles differently on different terrain: mud, gravel, tarmac.
  • Separate braking and coasting from throttle, and enforce a real stop speed.
  • Fix airborne handling so the car keeps its momentum and has some control in the air.
  • Test the controller on several cars to prove it works as a universal setup.
  • Add visual feedback so the player can see the wheels respond to their input and interact with the world.
