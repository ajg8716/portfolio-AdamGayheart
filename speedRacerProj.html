Speedracer Inspired Driving

Acrobatic driving mechanics built on top of Unreal Chaos Vehicles.

solo

Duration

My role
Gameplay Programmer

The goal

I am a fan of the Speed Racer franchise and love the driving in the 2008 film. I want to recreate it as a practice project using C++ in Unreal Engine 5.

What it shows

I designed, planned, and implemented mechanics on top of the Unreal Engine 5 Chaos Vehicle system to add physics-based acrobatic driving.

This project is a work in progress, last updated. Currently, I have implemented a four-wheel drift. The player toggles this on with a button press, engaging steering on the front and rear tires. Crab-walking the car around turns without losing speed or traction.
